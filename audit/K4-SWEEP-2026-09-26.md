## rental business (`/Users/shanelabounty/Projects/rental business`, paths under `apps/web/`)

- **K4-R1 HIGH** `lib/leases/party-change-builder.ts:156-160` — `startPartyChange` loads `incomingApplicantIds` with an
  unscoped `applicant.findMany`, then copies them onto this lease as Tenants and signers. A manager of A can pull B's
  applicant PII and put that person on A's lease. Fix: filter by the lease's property/application.
- **K4-R2 HIGH** `lib/units/actions.ts:113-126` — `updateUnit(propertyId, unitId)` checks `unit.write` on `propertyId`
  but never that `existing.propertyId === propertyId`. A manager of A rewrites B's unit (status, rent); the audit row lands on A.
- **K4-R3 HIGH** `lib/scope/current-scope.ts:42`, ~50 call sites (`app/(admin)/leases/[id]/page.tsx:158-161`,
  `confidential/[id]`, `api/reports/rent-roll`, `api/reports/tax-export`, money/*, reports/*, messages/*) — pages are
  guarded with `requireScope('lease.read' | 'ledger.read' | 'confidential.read' …)` then call `currentScope(actor)`, which
  defaults to `property.read`. A portfolio-wide maintenance tech with a manager grant on one house reads every lease,
  rent roll, tax export and confidential case. Fix: pass the guarding permission to `currentScope` (the SEC-03 rule,
  applied to reads).
- MED: `deposits/actions.ts:86-87,107-108` (foreign workOrderId/inspectionItemId embed B's documents in A's deposit
  packet); `workorders/actions.ts:105,137` (foreign turnoverProjectId can satisfy B's re-key safety gate);
  `accommodations/actions.ts:132,158` (foreign tenantId gets A's determination); `violations/actions.ts:334,346-349`
  (foreign screened applicant legitimizes an occupant); `messages/unrouted/page.tsx:29-33` + `comms/actions.ts:150-187`
  (unrouted inbox unscoped).
- LOW: `rubs-actions.ts:49,91` documentId unchecked; `property-expenses/actions.ts:52-59` entity-wide expense by a
  one-house grant; `confidential/actions.ts`, `scra/actions.ts` dangling foreign ids; `*ForWrite` helpers load unscoped
  then redirect to `/no-access` (existence oracle); `operational/actions.ts:128-148` audit cites unverified workOrderId;
  `billing/actions.ts:36,59` Stripe re-syncs under `ledger.read`.

- **K4-C1 HIGH** `src/notes/service.ts:82-103` — `createProgressNote` takes `clinicianId` from the appointment as proof of
  the treating relationship, and any clinician can book an appointment for any client with themselves as clinician
