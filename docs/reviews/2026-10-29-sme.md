## 2. Ranking and health critique

- **Hot, gravity 45000:** with median post net 4, p90 14 (fixture), the log term is ≤1.15 while a day of age is 1.92. Hot ≈ New with jitter; the DEMO shows net 0 at 10 h over net 14 at 27 h. For tens of posts/day I'd test gravity 90000–150000 (one decade = 25–42 h) so a 10-vote post survives a day. Measure first: median time a post spends in the top 5 of Hot vs New, and rank correlation Hot↔New per board at 6-h steps. Record as GH-08 (crossover at gravity 90000 = 90000 s) under a D- entry; the oracle already swaps constants cleanly (gravity 90000 proof in EVAL).
- **Best:** see #13; measure the fraction of sibling groups with all n ≤ 2 (fixture: most). With that, Wilson adds nothing; consider `top` with `created asc` tiebreak for n≤2 groups.
- **Gates:** 24 h is fine; 3 contributions is weak (char-min 1). Measure: contributions per account at first downvote in the fixture; set `min_contribution_chars` 40 (reuse `duplicate.min_chars`). Young limits (1 post/h, 2 comments/min) fit a help board; regular 6 comments/min is generous for humans and cheap for scripts — measure the p99 inter-comment gap of `abuse:normal` and set max to that ×2.
- Keep the discipline: these are hypotheses to measure, not "better".

## 5. Three things done right

1. Gated/self votes are **recorded, not dropped** — the detector reads what the counter ignores (`VoteEvent`), the pattern most forums lose by rejecting at the door.
2. **Signals for humans, never auto-action**, with the state machines and reasons as data and an append-only, totally ordered log (D-49/D-56) that survives org deletion.
3. **One door, one writer, one renderer with lint** — and the honest finding that the edit path is a second door is only visible because the rule exists.
