# PRD 5.3 — Ranking measurement study (measure only)

**Status:** approved 2026-11-01 (Shane: "A"); built the same day — `tools/rank_study.py`, results in `docs/EVAL.md` §Phase 5. Governed by D-59 (Phase 5 scope) and D-63 (measure only; any constant change is a separate owner decision with a rule-8 exception).

## Problem

Commons ranks with reddit's historical constants (`rules/ranking.json`: hot gravity 45,000 s; Best = Wilson lower bound, z 1.96) and the author's fixed +1 counts in both (D-12). Nobody has measured what those choices do on a homelab-sized board. Two reviewer claims need numbers, not opinions:

1. **Hot may be ordering ≈ New.** Median post net in the fixture is 4 and p90 is 14, so the log term is ≤ 1.15 while one day of age is worth 1.92; `docs/DEMO.md` Act 1 already shows net 0 at 10 h above net 14 at 27 h. (SME §2)
2. **Best on small n is mostly the tiebreak.** With the author's +1, every new comment starts at wilson(1,1) = 0.2065; one downvote → 0.095, one upvote → 0.342. PO count on the fixture: 1,941 of 5,000 comments have zero non-author votes; 3,912 have ≤ 2. Where every sibling has n ≤ 2, Best reduces to D-9's `(created desc, id asc)`. (SME #13)

The project refuses to say "fair" or "good ordering" without a number. This PRD produces the numbers and stops.

## Scope

A stdlib-Python oracle script, `tools/rank_study.py` (`python3 -I`), run over the frozen fixture at the fixture instant (NOW = T0 + 7 d) and at 6-hour steps back through the fixture's span, printing a report and writing `docs/EVAL.md` §Phase 5 numbers by hand (no file under `fixtures/` is written — rule 8).

Measurements, each defined so a reader can recompute it:

| # | Measurement | Definition | What it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Hot↔New rank agreement per board | Spearman ρ and Kendall τ between the Hot order and the New order of each board's visible posts, at each 6 h step; median and range across steps | Is Hot distinguishable from New at gravity 45,000? |
| M2 | Time in the top 5 | For each post, hours spent in a board's Hot top 5 vs New top 5 across the steps; distribution (median, p90) and the count of posts that reach Hot's top 5 but not New's | Does Hot surface anything New would not? |
| M3 | Sibling groups where Best is the tiebreak | Share of sibling groups (children of one parent, ≥ 2 members) where every member has n ≤ 2; same with n ≤ 3; share of comments whose only vote is the author's | How often is Best doing anything? |
| M4 | Best with and without the author's +1 | Re-rank every sibling group with `n = ups − 1 + downs` (author's +1 excluded from Best only, counted in Hot and Top); Kendall τ against the current Best order; count of groups whose first comment changes | What GW-09 would change |
| M5 | Gravity sweep | M1 and M2 recomputed at gravity 45,000 / 90,000 / 150,000 (one decade of votes = 12.5 h / 25 h / 41.7 h) | What GH-08 would change; the crossover offset is `log10(50/5) × gravity` by construction |
| M6 | Contributions at first downvote | For each fixture user who ever downvotes, their contribution count and account age at the first downvote; distribution; how many first downvotes fall within 24 h + 3 contributions ± 1 of the gate | Is the 24 h / 3-contribution gate (`rules/limits.json`) near the fixture's real behaviour? (Feeds 5.2's `aged_brigade` thresholds) |
| M7 | Inter-action gaps | p50 / p99 of the gap between consecutive comments and votes per non-abusive fixture user | Is 6 comments / min (regular) near human pace? (Feeds any later window change) |

Proposed golden cases, **written into `tools/golden_answers.py` as `@pending` and not asserted** until a D- entry adopts them: GH-08 (crossover at gravity 90,000 = 90,000 s), GW-09 (Best without the author's +1 for 1/1 vs 2/2 vs 1/2).

Report: `docs/EVAL.md` §Phase 5 "Ranking measured" with every number next to its command; one short paragraph per measurement saying what it means and what it does not.

## Non-goals (this PRD)

- Changing `rules/ranking.json`, `rules/limits.json`, any expected file or the fixture generator. D-63.
- Claiming Hot or Best is "better" at any setting. The study reports agreement, time-in-top, and shares; "better" needs a goal the owner has not set.
- Live data. The fixture is synthetic; the scope banner applies to every number.
- A UI. Nothing on the pages changes.
